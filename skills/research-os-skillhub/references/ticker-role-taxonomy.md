# Ticker Role Taxonomy

Use this reference before company mapping.

Canonical detail file:

```text
skill_hub/references/ticker_role_taxonomy.md
```

## Roles

- `focal_beneficiary`: direct thesis object.
- `supplier`: supplies the scarce capability or input.
- `customer`: validates demand, not necessarily the beneficiary.
- `peer`: comparable company.
- `comparison`: analogy or valuation comparison.
- `supply_chain_context`: useful for mapping, not direct thesis.
- `risk_object`: company or segment that may be hurt.
- `context_only`: background mention.
- `unknown`: role cannot be determined.

## Direct Thesis Candidates

Only these should directly drive company-level conclusions:

- `focal_beneficiary`
- `supplier`
- `risk_object`

Other roles need caution or validation.
