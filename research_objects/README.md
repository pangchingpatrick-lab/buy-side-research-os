# Research Objects

Research Objects are living investment research files.

The Research OS should not treat research as a one-time report. Each research topic, company, or investment theme should be maintained as a living Research Object that can be updated as new signals, evidence, hypotheses, decisions, and monitoring data arrive.

Reports are snapshots generated from Research Objects. A PM brief, investment memo, full report, or PPT should communicate the current state of a Research Object, not replace the underlying research process.

## How Research Objects Fit the OS

1. Data Collector updates signals and evidence.
2. Knowledge and thinking layers use the object to organize reusable industry, company, source, and account knowledge.
3. Hypotheses are generated, attacked, strengthened, or rejected inside the object.
4. Decisions are recorded as the current PM-facing state: long, short, watch, ignore, or needs more work.
5. Report Writer reads Research Objects to generate PM-facing communication outputs.
6. Monitoring updates the object after catalysts, earnings, filings, new signals, or thesis invalidation events.

## Template

Use `templates/research_object_template.yaml` when creating a new Research Object.

Do not use the template to invent research. Fill fields only with sourced data, verified facts, clearly labeled early signals, or explicit placeholders such as `待补充`, `待验证`, or `数据暂缺`.

## Operating Principle

The Research Object is the source of truth. Reports are downstream views.
