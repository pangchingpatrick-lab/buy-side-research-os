# Consumer Electronics Framework

## 1. Industry Essence

Consumer electronics monetize hardware replacement cycles, device ASP, component content, ecosystem services, and manufacturing scale. The buy-side question is whether a product cycle changes unit volume, ASP, mix, margin, or supplier content value.

## 2. Core Growth Drivers

- Replacement cycle and installed base age.
- New product category or feature upgrade.
- ASP and premium mix.
- Channel inventory and sell-through.
- Component innovation: displays, sensors, batteries, cameras, chips, AI features.
- Geographic demand, subsidy, carrier promotion.
- Ecosystem services attachment.

## 3. Business Model

Common models:

- Device OEM: units sold x ASP.
- Component supplier: content per device x unit volume.
- ODM / EMS: manufacturing volume x service fee or margin.
- Platform ecosystem: installed base x service ARPU.
- Accessories: attach rate x ASP.

## 4. Core Revenue/Profit Formula

Core formulas:

- Device revenue = sales volume x ASP
- Component revenue = device volume x content value per device
- Manufacturing revenue = units produced x fee / ASP share
- Services revenue = active installed base x ARPU
- Gross profit = revenue x product mix margin - warranty / logistics / manufacturing cost

Key sensitivities:

- Sell-through units.
- ASP and premium mix.
- Bill of materials cost.
- Component content value.
- Inventory digestion.
- Launch timing.

## 5. Key Operating Metrics

- Shipments, sell-through, market share.
- ASP, product mix, gross margin.
- Channel inventory, lead times, order revisions.
- Installed base, upgrade rate, attach rate.
- Component content per device.
- Production build plans and utilization.

## 6. Value Chain / Supply Chain

Value chain:

1. Component suppliers: chips, displays, sensors, batteries, camera, casing.
2. Module makers and assemblers.
3. OEM brand and operating system ecosystem.
4. Channels, carriers, retailers.
5. Consumers and enterprise buyers.
6. Services, accessories, app ecosystem.

Questions:

- Is the signal about sell-through, build plan, component content, or channel inventory?
- Which supplier has incremental content gain?
- Does feature innovation create paid demand or only cost inflation?

## 7. Listed Company Mapping

| Role | Revenue mechanism | Evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device OEM | Units x ASP | Sell-through, ASP, mix, margin |
| Component supplier | Content value x device volume | Design win, content share, order data |
| Assembler / ODM | Build volume x service fee | Build plan, utilization, customer mix |
| Ecosystem platform | Installed base x service ARPU | Active devices, services revenue, attach rate |
| Potential loser | Old component displaced by new architecture | Design loss, order cuts |

## 8. Financial Impact Pathway

Signal -> product cycle / feature adoption -> units, ASP, content, or attach rate -> revenue and margin -> inventory and earnings revision.

Examples:

- AI smartphone feature -> upgrade demand -> unit growth or premium mix -> OEM revenue and supplier content.
- New display or camera module -> content per device -> supplier revenue.
- Channel inventory high -> order cuts -> margin pressure.

## 9. Consensus vs Variant View

Consensus may price a new product cycle before sell-through proof. Variant view should test:

- Whether demand is replacement-driven or truly incremental.
- Whether ASP uplift offsets higher BOM cost.
- Whether supplier content gain is already reflected in orders.
- Whether build plans exceed sell-through.
- Whether AI or new features change upgrade behavior.

## 10. Risks and Invalidation Triggers

- Weak sell-through after launch.
- Channel inventory build.
- ASP pressure or promotion intensity.
- Component cost inflation.
- Design loss or content share loss.
- Product delay or quality issue.
- Macro pressure on discretionary demand.

## 11. Buy-Side Investment Judgment Framework

- Long candidate: verified product-cycle demand, favorable ASP or content mix, and supplier/OEM exposure not fully priced.
- Watchlist: strong launch signal but sell-through and orders not yet verified.
- Too early / Need more evidence: feature signal lacks demand proof.
- Avoid: replacement cycle weak or margin impact unclear.
- Short candidate: market prices upgrade cycle but evidence shows inventory build, weak sell-through, or ASP pressure.
