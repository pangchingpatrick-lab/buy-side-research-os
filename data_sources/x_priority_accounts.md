## Signal Standard

A post can enter the core research packet only if it is:

- relevant to the research question
- specific about a company, product, technology, customer, pricing, demand, supply, capex, order, or bottleneck
- useful for investment research
- score >= 18/25 under the signal scoring rules
- marked `早期信号，待验证` unless verified by a primary source
