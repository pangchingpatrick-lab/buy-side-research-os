## Candidate Pattern 1: AI Infra Bottleneck Watchlist

Reusable lesson:

When local AI data is dominated by X, specialist posts, and secondary research summaries, the right output is usually a bottleneck watchlist rather than a trade call.

Reusable routing change:

- Primary skill should be bottleneck mapping when the question is theme-level.
- Research event distillation should be auxiliary whenever X / public specialist sources materially affect the report.
- Financial validation bridge must run before any idea upgrades from watchlist to long candidate.

General or industry-specific:

- General method, but especially useful for AI infrastructure, semiconductor, datacenter, power, and other capex-heavy supply chains.

## Candidate Pattern 2: Bottleneck Migration Is More Valuable Than Broad AI Beta

Reusable lesson:

The strongest local AI signals are not broad statements like "AI demand is strong." They are signals that specify which layer is becoming constrained: memory bandwidth, interconnect, optical networking, burn-in / test, rack-scale cooling, power, or cloud financing.

How future routing should change:

- Route broad AI packets through constrained-layer ranking before company mapping.
- Require each report-driving event to identify `demand shock -> constrained layer -> scarce capability -> beneficiary mapping -> financial line item`.
- Treat generic semiconductor rally, upgrades, options flow, and ETF movement as market context unless they also contain research evidence.

General or industry-specific:

- Industry-specific to AI infrastructure, but portable to other bottleneck-driven industries.

## Candidate Pattern 3: Context Ticker Discipline

Reusable lesson:

Tickers mentioned in an AI infrastructure post are not automatically beneficiaries. MSFT, GOOGL, NOW, SPX, SOXL, and customer names often function as demand validators, benchmark context, or trading context rather than focal investment signals.

How future routing should change:

- Preserve ticker_role in every event.
- Only `focal_beneficiary` and clearly identified `supplier` roles should feed company-research conclusions.
- Customer, peer, benchmark, and context tickers should be demoted unless the report explicitly explains why they are investable.

General or industry-specific:

- General pattern across all sectors.
