# Skill 006: Alpha Migration

## 1. Skill purpose

Identify where the investable opportunity is moving along the value chain as narratives, constraints, and profit pools change.

## 2. When to use

Use when:

- the first-order winner is already well known
- value is migrating from one layer to another
- a bottleneck, pricing shift, or technology transition changes profit pools
- the research question asks where the next alpha may be
- consensus may still focus on the old beneficiary

## 3. Input required

- original market narrative
- current consensus beneficiaries
- value-chain map
- new signal or constraint
- company exposure
- pricing, margin, order, or capex evidence

## 4. Reasoning steps

1. Identify the original alpha source.
2. Identify why that alpha may be maturing, priced in, or shifting.
3. Map the value chain and profit pools.
4. Identify the new scarce or underappreciated node.
5. Determine whether the new node has pricing power, operating leverage, or estimate revision potential.
6. Compare old beneficiary vs new beneficiary.
7. Define evidence that confirms migration.
8. Define risks that stop migration.

## 5. Output expected

- old alpha location
- new potential alpha location
- value-chain migration map
- company beneficiaries and losers
- financial translation
- consensus gap
- validation and invalidation data

## 6. Example use case

AI infrastructure alpha may migrate from GPUs to HBM, networking, optical interconnect, data center power, or grid equipment as the obvious compute winner becomes priced in and constraints move upstream.

## 7. Common mistakes

- assuming alpha always moves upstream
- ignoring valuation and consensus ownership
- confusing revenue growth with alpha
- failing to identify the catalyst for market recognition
- ignoring whether the new node can capture economics
