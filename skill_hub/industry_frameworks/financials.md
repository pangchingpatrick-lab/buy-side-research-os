# Financials Fintech Framework

## Boundary of this framework

- This industry framework defines the industry map, value chain, business models, core metrics, and key risks.
- It should not determine the final reasoning path.
- The final reasoning path should be selected from `skill_hub/research_methodologies/`.
- Industry framework answers "what to look at".
- Research methodology answers "how to think".

## Industry Essence

Financials and fintech monetize money movement, credit intermediation, risk pooling, transactions, assets, and financial infrastructure.

## Core Formula

- Bank revenue = earning assets x NIM + fee income
- Payments revenue = transaction volume x take rate + service fees
- Wealth revenue = AUM x fee rate
- Broker / exchange revenue = trading volume x fee rate + data / clearing fees
- Insurance profit = premiums - claims - expenses + investment income

## Key Metrics

- transaction volume, TPV, take rate
- loan book, deposits, NIM, NPL ratio, provisions
- AUM, net flows, fee rate
- trading volume and market share
- premiums, combined ratio, loss ratio
- capital adequacy and liquidity

## Value Chain

Distribution -> underwriting / compliance / risk -> payment rails / licenses / custody -> balance sheet or capital partners -> spread / fee / take-rate monetization.

## Buy-Side Questions

- Is revenue volume-driven, spread-driven, fee-driven, or balance-sheet-driven?
- Who holds credit risk?
- Is take rate sustainable?
- Is NIM benefit offset by funding cost?
- Is regulation a cap or a moat?

## Output To Report

- revenue driver decomposition
- credit / funding / regulatory risk
- operating leverage
- valuation and ROE sensitivity
