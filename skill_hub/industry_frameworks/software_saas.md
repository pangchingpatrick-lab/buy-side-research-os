# Software SaaS Framework

This is a buy-side software and SaaS industry framework. It is designed for SaaS earnings, AI software, enterprise software, cybersecurity, and cloud data platform analysis.

Use it after external data has already been collected, classified, and source-tagged.

## 1. Industry Essence

Software and SaaS businesses are driven by recurring revenue, customer retention, pricing power, product adoption, sales efficiency, and operating leverage.

The investable question is not "software is high margin." The investable question is:

- Is growth durable?
- Is retention strong enough to compound revenue?
- Is the product mission-critical or discretionary?
- Is AI improving monetization or increasing cost?
- Is valuation already pricing perfect execution?

Core insight chain:

```text
Product adoption
-> customer growth / usage / expansion
-> ARR and revenue growth
-> retention and sales efficiency
-> margin and FCF leverage
-> valuation rerating or compression
```

## 2. Main Sub-Sectors

Buy-side focus:

- cloud workload growth

Endpoint, network, cloud, identity, data security, and security operations software.

Buy-side focus:

- platform consolidation
- breach-driven demand
- net retention
- module attach
- enterprise budget resilience
- competition and pricing pressure

### Data Infrastructure

Databases, data warehouses, lakehouses, analytics, observability data layers, and AI data platforms.

Buy-side focus:

- consumption growth
- workload migration
- customer expansion
- AI data workload attachment
- storage / compute economics

### DevOps

Developer tools, CI/CD, code hosting, observability, incident management, and platform engineering.

Buy-side focus:

- developer adoption
- seat expansion

### AI Software

AI-native software, copilots, AI workflow automation, model operations, and AI-enabled enterprise tools.

Buy-side focus:

Industry-specific software for healthcare, finance, construction, legal, real estate, logistics, and other vertical workflows.

### Consumer Subscription Software

Consumer productivity, creative, education, fitness, media, and app subscriptions.

Buy-side focus:

- marketing efficiency
- pricing power

## 3. Core Formulas

```text
ARR = customers x ACV
Revenue = ARR + usage-based revenue + services revenue
Net retention = expansion - contraction - churn
Rule of 40 = revenue growth + FCF margin
```

Additional formulas:

| Formula | Use |
| --- | --- |
| New ARR = new customers x starting ACV | Tests new-logo engine. |
| Expansion ARR = existing customers x expansion rate | Tests upsell and product breadth. |
| Churned ARR = churned customers x ACV | Tests product durability. |
| Gross profit = revenue x gross margin | Tests software margin structure. |
| FCF = operating cash flow - capex | Tests real economic output. |
| CAC payback = CAC / gross profit from new ARR | Tests sales efficiency. |

## 5. Value Chain

Software value chain:

```text
Cloud infrastructure
-> data platforms
-> application software
-> security layer
-> workflow automation
-> enterprise customers
```

Economic capture questions:

- Cloud infrastructure: Is software revenue dependent on third-party cloud cost?
- Data platforms: Does data gravity create retention?
- Application software: Does the company own the workflow?
- Security layer: Is demand resilient because risk is non-discretionary?
- Workflow automation: Is AI improving productivity and willingness to pay?
- Enterprise customers: Is the product tied to budget priority and measurable ROI?

## 7. Financial Impact Pathway

Software signals must be translated into financial mechanisms.

### ARR Growth

Signals:

- new product adoption
- new enterprise customers
- expansion deals
- cloud migration
- AI feature attach

Financial pathway:

```text
Product adoption -> customer count / ACV / usage -> ARR -> revenue growth -> valuation
```

Questions:

- Is ARR growth from new logos, expansion, usage, or price?
- Is ARR growth supported by billings and cRPO?

### NRR Improvement

Signals:

- module expansion
- usage growth
- higher-tier plans
- AI add-ons
- workflow consolidation

Financial pathway:

```text
NRR improvement -> lower required new-logo growth -> durable compounding -> higher multiple
```

Watch for:

- NRR masking weak new customer growth
- expansion slowing after initial adoption
- churn hidden by large customer upsell

### Pricing Power

Signals:

- price increase
- packaging change
- premium AI tier
- enterprise renewal uplift

Financial pathway:

```text
Price increase -> ARPU / ACV uplift -> revenue growth -> margin leverage
```

Risk:

- pricing pressure triggers churn or seat reduction.

### Margin Expansion

Margin expands when:

- gross margin is stable
- sales efficiency improves
- R&D and G&A scale
- cloud cost is optimized
- AI attach increases ARPU more than inference cost

Margin compresses when:

- cloud cost rises
- AI inference cost is high
- services mix increases
- sales productivity deteriorates
- price competition intensifies

### Sales Efficiency

Signals:

- CAC payback improves
- large deal cycle shortens
- pipeline converts faster
- partner/channel contribution rises

Financial pathway:

```text
Sales efficiency -> lower S&M burden -> operating margin -> FCF generation
```

### FCF Generation

FCF generation matters because software valuation depends on growth quality, not growth alone.

Watch:

- FCF margin
- SBC intensity
- billings quality
- deferred revenue
- cash conversion

### Valuation Rerating

Valuation rerates when:

- growth durability improves
- NRR stabilizes or reaccelerates
- margin expands without sacrificing growth
- AI creates monetizable uplift
- Rule of 40 improves
- consensus underestimates operating leverage

Valuation compresses when:

- growth slows
- NRR weakens
- billings or cRPO disappoint
- AI cost pressure emerges
- valuation already prices perfect execution

## 8. Buy-Side Questions

Use these questions to convert software signals into investment insight:

- Is growth durable?
- Is retention strong?
- Is AI improving monetization or increasing costs?
- Is usage-based revenue accelerating or slowing?
- Is sales efficiency improving?
- Is the company gaining share?
- Is valuation already pricing perfection?
- Is growth coming from customers, ACV, usage, or price?
- Is NRR hiding churn or weak new-logo growth?
- Are AI features paid, bundled, or defensive?
- Does the company own workflow, data, distribution, or budget?
- What would trigger a multiple rerating or compression?

## 9. Risks And Invalidation

Key risks:

- slowing growth
- churn increase
- weak NRR
- AI commoditization
- pricing pressure
- rising cloud costs
- sales efficiency deterioration
- competition
- valuation compression
- customer budget cuts
- usage slowdown
- seat compression from AI automation
- security or reliability incident

Invalidation triggers:

- ARR growth decelerates faster than expected
- NRR / NDR weakens
- gross retention declines
- billings or cRPO misses
- CAC payback deteriorates
- gross margin falls because of cloud or AI inference cost
- AI attach fails to produce paid uplift
- price increase causes churn or downgrade
- consensus estimates stop rising while valuation remains high

## 10. Investment Judgment Framework

Classify software/SaaS opportunities into one of five states.

### Long Candidate

Use when:

- growth is durable
- NRR and gross retention are strong
- pricing power is visible
- sales efficiency is improving
- margin / FCF leverage is credible
- valuation does not fully reflect quality or reacceleration
- catalyst is visible

Required evidence:

- ARR / revenue / billings / cRPO support
- retention and customer growth
- margin or FCF evidence
- consensus gap

### Watchlist

Use when:

- product adoption or AI attach is promising
- financial validation is incomplete
- valuation or timing is unclear
- growth quality needs one or two more quarters of evidence

Required next step:

- track ARR, NRR, billings, cRPO, gross margin, and AI monetization.

### Too Early / Need More Evidence

Use when:

- evidence is mostly product launch, demo, social signal, or anecdote
- AI monetization is unproven
- customer adoption is not yet visible in ARR or usage
- margin impact is unclear

Required next step:

- wait for customer, ARR, usage, retention, or margin data.

### Avoid

Use when:

- growth slows without margin offset
- retention quality weakens
- valuation is high
- product differentiation is unclear
- AI features are defensive and not monetizable

Required next step:

- avoid PM action until risk/reward changes.

### Short Candidate

Use when:

- market prices durable growth or AI uplift
- evidence shows slowing usage, weak NRR, margin pressure, sales inefficiency, or valuation overreach
- downside catalyst is visible

Required evidence:

- deterioration in ARR, billings, cRPO, NRR, margin, or sales efficiency
- consensus estimates vulnerable to cuts
- valuation still optimistic
