# Consensus Gap Method

Use this method to identify what the market believes and where the variant view may differ.

## Inputs

- market price action
- broker consensus
- earnings revisions
- valuation multiples
- management guidance
- social and media narratives

## Steps

1. State what consensus likely believes.
2. Identify what is already priced in.
3. Define the non-consensus view.
4. Explain why consensus may be wrong.
5. Identify where alpha may migrate from and to.
6. Identify why the gap may close now rather than last year or next year.
7. Map the variant view to 3-5 listed-company expressions.
8. Identify what evidence would close or invalidate the gap.

## Guardrails

- If consensus data is unavailable, write `市场预期待验证`.
- Do not claim the market is wrong without a falsifiable reason.
- Separate sentiment from earnings expectations.
- Do not include candidates that only express the consensus view.
- Separate alpha candidates from risk/context watches.
- Do not include high-conviction candidates without a timing catalyst.
- If timing is unclear, mark `timing catalyst 待验证`.
