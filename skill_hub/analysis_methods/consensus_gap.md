# Consensus Gap Method

Use this method to identify what the market believes and where the variant view may differ.

## Inputs

- market price action
- broker consensus
- earnings revisions
- valuation multiples
- management guidance
- social and media narratives

## Steps

1. State what consensus likely believes.
2. Identify what is already priced in.
3. Define the non-consensus view.
4. Explain why consensus may be wrong.
5. Identify what evidence would close or invalidate the gap.

## Output

| Consensus belief | Evidence | Variant view | Why market may be wrong | Data needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | | |

## Guardrails

- If consensus data is unavailable, write `市场预期待验证`.
- Do not claim the market is wrong without a falsifiable reason.
- Separate sentiment from earnings expectations.
