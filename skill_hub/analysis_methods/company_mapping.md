# Company Mapping Method

Use this method to map a theme or signal to listed companies.

## Inputs

- research topic
- industry framework
- related products or technologies
- value-chain location
- verified and unverified evidence

## Steps

1. Identify the value-chain node affected by the signal.
2. List companies by direct, indirect, negative, or uncertain exposure.
3. Tie every company to a revenue or profit mechanism.
4. Rank by expected earnings sensitivity and evidence quality.
5. Mark unsupported ticker-level claims as `待验证`.

## Output

| Rank | Company | Ticker | Exposure type | Value-chain role | Mechanism | Evidence quality | Verification status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | Direct / Indirect / Negative / Watchlist | | | High / Medium / Low | |

## Guardrails

- Do not add tickers without source support.
- Do not confuse narrative relevance with earnings exposure.
- Separate direct beneficiaries from second-order beneficiaries.
