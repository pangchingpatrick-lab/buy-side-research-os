# Company Mapping Method

Use this method to map a theme or signal to listed companies.

## Inputs

- research topic
- industry framework
- related products or technologies
- value-chain location
- verified and unverified evidence

## Steps

1. Identify the value-chain node affected by the signal.
2. List companies by direct, indirect, negative, or uncertain exposure.
3. Tie every company to a revenue or profit mechanism.
4. Build a peer / proxy set for every serious candidate.
5. Explain why the selected ticker is a better or worse expression than those peers/proxies.
6. Identify why now / timing catalyst for every serious candidate.
7. Rank by expected earnings sensitivity, exposure purity, peer displacement, timing catalyst, and evidence quality.
8. Mark unsupported ticker-level claims as `待验证`.

## Output

| Rank | Company | Ticker | Exposure type | Value-chain role | Peer / proxy set | Why this / why not peers | Why now / timing catalyst | Mechanism | Evidence quality | Verification status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| | | | Direct / Indirect / Negative / Watchlist | | | | | | High / Medium / Low | |

## Guardrails

- Do not add tickers without source support.
- Do not confuse narrative relevance with earnings exposure.
- Separate direct beneficiaries from second-order beneficiaries.
- Do not stop at company description. Answer why this company is the right stock expression versus peers/proxies.
- If the peer comparison is not possible from available data, mark `peer comparison 待补充` and do not overstate conviction.
- If timing is not clear, mark `timing catalyst 待验证` and do not overstate conviction.
